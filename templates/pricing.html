Common Questions
What happens when Pro expires?
Your account moves to Free automatically. All your data — trips, diary, expenses — stays safe. You lose access to Pro features until you renew.
Is there auto-renewal?
Never. Yaply does not auto-charge you. Every payment is manual. You'll get a reminder before Pro expires so you can renew if you want.
Can I use a promo code?
Yes. Enter your code above, hit Apply, then click Get Pro. The discount applies automatically before you pay.
Weekly vs Monthly — which is better?
Monthly at ₹399 gives 30 full days. Weekly is ₹99×4 = ₹396 for the same month — almost the same price but Monthly is simpler. For short trips, weekly is perfect.
