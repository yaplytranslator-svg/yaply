Common Questions
What happens when my Pro expires?
Your account automatically moves to Free tier. You keep all your trips and diary data. You just lose access to Pro-only features until you renew.
Is there auto-renewal?
No. Yaply does not auto-charge you. Each payment is manual. You will receive a notification before your Pro expires so you can renew if you want.
Can I use a promo code with any plan?
Yes. Enter your code above before clicking Get Pro. The discount applies automatically to your next payment.
Weekly vs Monthly — which is better?
Monthly at ₹399 is almost the same as weekly ₹99×4=₹396 — but you get 30 full days. Monthly is almost always the better choice.
