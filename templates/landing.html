+
Yaply uses the most advanced AI models (Groq with Llama) to generate detailed, personalised itineraries. All prices are verified against real data and you can edit every detail after generation.
What makes Yaply's AI translator different?
+
Yaply combines DeepL (world's most accurate translation engine) with AI voice recognition. It works in real time for live conversations and supports 50+ languages including Japanese, Arabic, Hindi, Chinese and more.

+
Yes! The live budget tracker updates every time you edit your itinerary. It shows spending by day, remaining budget, per-person costs and gives you alerts if you're going over budget.

No! You can start completely free and plan up to 5 trips per month. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited trips, live translation, camera scanning, scam alerts and all premium features.

+
Absolutely. The door-to-door journey feature finds your nearest airport and shows you exactly how to reach it by train, bus or cab — even from towns with no direct transport links.
